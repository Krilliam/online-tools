URL Encoder / Decoder

Encode or decode URLs, query strings, and form data with proper UTF-8 support.

How to use

  1. Select the operation (Encode or Decode)
  2. Choose the encoding mode based on your use case
  3. Paste your text in the Input field
  4. The result appears automatically in the Output field
  5. Use "Copy" or "Download" to save the result

Tip: Enable "Process line by line" to encode/decode multiple URLs at once.

Encoding modes explained:
  • URL Component: Encodes everything except A-Z a-z 0-9 - _ . ! ~ * ' ( ). Best for query parameters.
  • Full URL: Preserves structural characters like :/?#[]@. Best for complete URLs.
  • Form URL-encoded: Spaces become + instead of %20. Used in HTML form submissions.
